How obu works

Three steps. You set it up once, and it runs on its own.

  1. 1

    Choose what to block, and when.

    Pick the apps you open out of habit. Each block you set is called a guard, and it runs one of three ways.

    • Quick sessionsBlock now, until a timer ends. For reading, a workout, or dinner.
    • SchedulesThe same hours every week. Work 9–5, or nights 11–7.
    • LimitsA daily cap per app: 30 minutes, or 10 opens.
  2. 2

    obu steps in when you reach for one.

    Open a blocked app and you see obu's screen instead. It shows what's left for today, and the reason you gave for cutting back.

    You said:Be there at dinner

  3. 3

    See the time you win back.

    obu counts the hours your guards held, and calls them hours back. They add up for good: a bad day never takes any away. As they grow, the orb in the app changes form. There are 25 to find.

Will it actually stop me?

Yes, as firmly as you choose. You pick one strictness mode for the whole app, while you're thinking clearly. In Normal and Hardcore, turning a guard off takes a wait that outlasts the urge.

Questions people ask first

When can I use it?

obu hasn't launched yet. The TestFlight beta opens soon, and we won't promise a date we can't keep. Join the waitlist and you get one email when it opens.

How is it different from Apple's Screen Time?

A Screen Time limit ends with one tap on Ignore Limit. In obu's Normal and Hardcore modes, turning a guard off takes a 15-minute or 24-hour wait, and obu shows you the hours you win back.

What do I get for free?

One guard, Relax and Normal modes, your hours-back count, and all 25 orb forms. The free plan never expires. Plus adds unlimited guards, Hardcore mode, the 30-day challenge, and Family.

How does the 14-day trial work?

The trial of Plus needs a card. We remind you on day 12. Cancel before day 14 and you pay nothing.

What happens on a bad day?

Nothing is taken away. Your hours back and every orb form you've earned stay yours.

Can obu see which apps I use?

No. obu works through Apple's Screen Time, and your usage data never leaves your iPhone. Not even we can see it.

Can I use it with family or friends?

Yes. Invite up to five people and follow each other's progress. They never see your apps or minutes. The Family plan adds parent mode for kids' phones.

What if I truly need a blocked app?

There is an emergency stop: all guards off for 24 hours, once every 30 days. Outside of that, turning a guard off takes the wait your mode sets.

Is there an Android version?

iPhone first. Android comes later.
